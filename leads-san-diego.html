THE SCRIPTThirty seconds, then shut up.

Opener
“Hi, is this [Name]? — Quick one, I'll be 30 seconds. I run TideMerge, we build AI receptionists for small firms. Reason I'm calling: if somebody gets arrested Friday at 11 PM and calls your office, what happens to that call right now?”

Then stop talking. Their answer is the whole pitch. They'll say voicemail, an answering service, or “it forwards to my cell.” Whatever they say, you now know exactly what you're selling against.

The three answers
“Goes to voicemail.”
“Right — and four out of five people who hit voicemail never call back. They dial the next firm. So that's not a missed call, that's a case that went to your competitor. What's an average case worth to you?”

Let them say the number out loud. Then let it sit.

“I have an answering service.”
“How much are you paying them? And honestly — how many of those messages are worth calling back? Most services take a name and a number. Mine qualifies the caller, asks your intake questions, and books them on your calendar. In your voice.”
“It forwards to my cell.”
“So you're the receptionist. When you're in a deposition or asleep, does it still ring? And when you do pick up at 11 PM — is that the version of you you want signing a client?”
The close — sell the demo, not the subscription
“I'm not going to pitch you over the phone. Let me build one trained on your firm and send you a number. Call it yourself, hear what your intake sounds like at 2 AM. Takes me a day. If it's not good, delete my email and we're done.”

Get the email. That's the win condition. Don't quote a price on call one — you have five days to go live and a free demo, and those are stronger than any number you could say.

Objections you'll actually hear
“My clients don't want to talk to a robot.”

“Agreed — that's exactly why it sounds like you and not a phone tree. Let me build it and you tell me if you can spot it. If you can, I've got nothing to sell you.”

“I have ethics rules about intake.”

Real concern, don't wave it off. “It doesn't give legal advice and it doesn't take confidential facts — it qualifies, screens conflicts on your criteria, and books. You approve every question it asks. Want to see the script?”

“Send me some info.”

“I'll do better than a PDF — I'll send you a number to call. Best email?”

“How much?”

“Depends on call volume and I don't want to quote you blind. Hear it first.”

Timing & compliance

Call 8:00–9:30 AM or after 4:30 PM. Mid-day they're in court and you'll get the gatekeeper.

Solo attorney numbers are often personal cells. B2B calls are generally exempt from the National DNC Registry, but sole proprietorships sit in a grey area — and attorneys are exactly the people who will call you on it. Scrub the list if you're scaling this past ten.

California is a two-party consent state. If you record calls for training, disclose it at the top.
